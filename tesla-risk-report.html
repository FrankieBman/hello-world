Management Read-Through Earnings call · 4 key tape-bombs

Margin recovery is real — gross margin printed a fourth consecutive quarter of expansion thanks to regulatory credits ($595M), FSD revenue recognition acceleration, and stable ASPs. Management reaffirmed positive YoY delivery growth despite the Q1 inventory build, attributing the miss to refresh-cycle changeover for Model Y and softer China demand.

Capex guidance was raised meaningfully to > $25B for FY26, with the bulk earmarked for AI training compute (Dojo + Nvidia), Cybercab tooling, and Optimus pilot lines. Free cash flow guide reiterated as positive but materially compressed.

Robotaxi paid launch confirmed for June 2026 across Austin and select Bay Area zones, with five-metro expansion targeted by YE26. NHTSA preliminary investigation acknowledged but described as cooperative. Optimus first-customer shipments still on track for 2027.

Verdict Summary Horizon · 12 months
Tesla earns a HOLD at a composite risk of 61/100. The bull thesis is intact — margins re-expanding, FCF positive, FSD compounding, and a real robotaxi launch on the calendar — but valuation does the punishing work. At 381× trailing earnings the stock prices in a near-flawless transition to an AI/mobility platform. The Q1 delivery miss and 27-day inventory build say execution is not yet flawless.

We would add on pullbacks toward $340 (200-DMA) where risk/reward improves, and trim above $470 where the multiple becomes indefensible without robotaxi proof points. Catalyst path is dense between now and YE26; conviction should track the June paid launch.
